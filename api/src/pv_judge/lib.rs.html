1//! Semantic judge (action-correctness layer).
2//!
3//! Deterministic gates (schema, policy rules, budgets) cannot answer
4//! questions such as "do these arguments fit the task this agent was
5//! given?". The judge answers them as probabilities over a fixed label
6//! set, and never decides: policy thresholds turn the probabilities
7//! into allow, ask, or deny. Every verdict, with its full distribution
8//! and the pinned model (name, version, weights SHA-256), is written
9//! into the signed audit trail so a decision can be replayed.
10//!
11//! Layers:
12//! * [`model`], [`predicate`], [`adapter`] — the data model, the named
13//!   and versioned predicates, the [`JudgeAdapter`] interface and output
14//!   validation;
15//! * [`thresholds`] — policy thresholds and failure behaviour;
16//! * [`record`] — what goes into the audit trail;
17//! * [`drift`] — the runtime calibration check;
18//! * `runner` (feature `runner`) — deadlines and the concurrency limit;
19//! * `mock`, `calibration` (feature `runner`) — the test double and the
20//!   build-time calibration harness;
21//! * `local` (feature `onnx`), `cloud` (feature `cloud`) — the backends.
