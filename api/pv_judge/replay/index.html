Module replay

Expand description

Replaying recorded decisions.

Every judged tool call carries a JudgeRecord: the verdicts (the full distribution or the failure class, per predicate), the predicates in ask-by-default mode at the time, and what the thresholds decided. Replaying rebuilds the judge’s outcomes from the record, as a mock judge answering with the recorded probabilities, and runs them through ThresholdPolicy::decide, the function the harness itself uses. Two uses:

  • an auditor checks that each recorded decision follows from its verdicts under the thresholds that were configured (“the judge said 0.82 mismatch, the threshold was 0.7, so it was denied”);
  • an operator tries other thresholds on shadow-mode or pilot traffic and sees which calls they would have blocked.

Only the thresholds are replayed. What a WASM policy (interface version 2) decided from the verdicts, policy_decision in the record, is not reproduced. A replay does not detect tampering either: check the receipt that covers the records first.
